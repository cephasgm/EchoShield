About EchoShield

The Simulated Battlefield

We re‑create Wi‑Fi (2.4 GHz OFDM), LoRa (868 MHz chirp), and DJI‑style frequency‑hopping protocols. The AI learns from synthetic IQ samples and spectrograms, enabling detection and jamming without any radio hardware.

Real‑World Ready

The same models and algorithms can be flashed onto software‑defined radios (HackRF, LimeSDR) for field use. Airport security, military, and critical infrastructure teams train with EchoShield before deployment.
