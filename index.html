Why EchoShield?

RF Simulation Engine

Generate realistic IQ samples for Wi‑Fi (OFDM, 64‑QAM), LoRa (chirp spread spectrum), and DJI frequency‑hopping protocols – all in Python without SDR hardware.

AI Detection & Classification

A convolutional neural network (CNN) analyzes spectrograms in real time, identifying the drone protocol with high confidence (>95% on synthetic data).

Smart Jamming Advisor

Once a threat is classified, EchoShield suggests the most effective jamming waveform: wideband noise, chirp jammer, or sweeping tones – tailored to the protocol.

Progressive Web App

Algorithm IP Ready

How It Works

1

Sign Up & Authenticate

Create an account using your email or Google. Your data is protected by Firebase Authentication and Firestore security rules.

2

Select a Drone Protocol

From the dashboard, choose between Wi‑Fi 2.4 GHz, LoRa 868 MHz, or DJI Ocusync. You can also request a custom synthetic scenario.

3

Run the Simulation

The Python backend generates a 1‑second IQ capture, computes a spectrogram, and sends it to the AI classifier.

4

Get AI Analysis & Jamming

The neural network identifies the protocol and returns a confidence score. The system then proposes an optimal jamming waveform.

5

Review & Export

Visualize the spectrogram and jamming parameters. Every run is logged to your history for further study or reporting.

Simulated Drone Protocols

Wi‑Fi (2.4 GHz)

OFDM with 64 subcarriers, 20 MHz bandwidth, 64‑QAM modulation – typical of many consumer drones. Jamming strategy: wideband noise.

LoRa 868 MHz

Chirp spread spectrum (SF7‑SF12), 125 kHz bandwidth. Long‑range, low‑power. Jamming strategy: synchronous chirp jammer.

DJI Ocusync

Proprietary frequency‑hopping over 2.4‑2.483 GHz. Our simulator emulates hopping patterns. Jamming strategy: sweeping CW tones.
